# THE LAST LEDGER — Episode 3: "Settling Accounts"

## Scene 1
INT. HARBORVIEW INN - OFFICE - DAY

Mara lays the ledger flat on the desk in front of Mr. Okonkwo. Her hands are
steady, unbandaged, turning pages quickly.

MARA
Every crate, every dollar, from the day we opened. Check it against the
manifest yourself.

## Scene 2
EXT. HARBORVIEW INN - DOCK - EVENING

Dev stands at the rail, checking the time on his grandfather's silver pocket
watch, open on its chain exactly as before — the same battered silver case,
the same worn engraving.

DEV
(closing the watch with a snap)
Right on schedule.

MARA
Since when did you start carrying that again?

DEV
(pocketing it, not really answering)
Some things you don't stay rid of.

## Scene 3
INT. HARBORVIEW INN - KITCHEN - NIGHT

Mara and Dev sit across from each other, the ledger closed between them, a
single lamp lit.

MARA
Okonkwo signed off. We're clean.

DEV
For now.

MARA
For now's all we ever get.

FADE OUT.

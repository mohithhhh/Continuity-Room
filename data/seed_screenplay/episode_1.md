# THE LAST LEDGER — Episode 1: "High Tide"

## Scene 1
INT. HARBORVIEW INN - KITCHEN - MORNING

MARA COLE (30s, sharp-eyed, sleeves rolled up) counts a stack of cash at the
counter. DEV PATEL (40s, quiet, wears a battered silver pocket watch on a
chain) leans in the doorway, watching her count.

MARA
Four thousand short. Again.

DEV
(touching the watch at his chest)
This was my grandfather's. He counted slower than you and never came up
short.

MARA
Sentimental men make bad bookkeepers.

## Scene 2
EXT. HARBORVIEW INN - DOCK - DAY

Mara and Dev walk along the dock. Gulls overhead. Dev's silver pocket watch
catches the light as he checks the time.

DEV
Low tide's in six hours. If the shipment isn't unloaded by then, we lose the
whole crate to the sandbar.

MARA
Then we'd better hope your watch runs fast.

## Scene 3
INT. HARBORVIEW INN - KITCHEN - NIGHT

Mara enters, her RIGHT HAND heavily bandaged, wincing. A stove burner still
glows behind her.

DEV
What happened?

MARA
Burner flared. Caught my right hand reaching for the kettle.
(she flexes her bandaged fingers, wincing)
I won't be much use with a pen for a while.

DEV
Sit. I'll finish the ledger tonight.

## Scene 4
INT. HARBORVIEW INN - OFFICE - NIGHT

Dev works alone at the ledger by lamplight, his grandfather's silver pocket
watch open on the desk beside him, ticking.

## Scene 5
EXT. RIVER BRIDGE - NIGHT

Dev stands alone at the railing. He takes off the silver pocket watch,
stares at it for a long beat, then hurls it into the river below. It sinks
without a sound.

DEV
(to himself)
No more counting.

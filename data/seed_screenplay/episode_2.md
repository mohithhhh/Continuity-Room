# THE LAST LEDGER — Episode 2: "Dry Spell"

## Scene 1
INT. HARBORVIEW INN - PARLOR - DAY

Mara sits at the old upright piano, playing a slow tune with BOTH HANDS,
fingers moving freely over the keys, no bandage, no wince.

DEV (O.S.)
You're playing again.

MARA
(not stopping)
Been meaning to. Feels good to use both hands.

## Scene 2
INT. HARBORVIEW INN - OFFICE - DAY

Dev sits across from a STRANGER, MR. OKONKWO (50s, insurance investigator).

MR. OKONKWO
The inn's books don't match the harbor manifest. Four thousand missing,
your partner says.

DEV
Mara handles the ledger. I just carry the crates.

## Scene 3
EXT. HARBOR - DOCK - DAY

Dev and Mara supervise dockworkers unloading crates. Dev checks nothing at
his wrist or chest — no watch, no gesture toward one.

MARA
Six hours to high tide. We're ahead of schedule for once.

## Scene 4
EXT. HARBORVIEW INN - PORCH - NIGHT

Rain hammers the porch roof. It has been raining for three straight days.
Mara watches the storm from the doorway, arms crossed.

MARA
Third day of this. Road out of town will be a swamp by morning.

## Scene 5
EXT. COASTAL ROAD - NIGHT

Later the same night. Dev drives an old truck down a bone-dry, dusty coastal
road, headlights cutting through still, clear air. Dust kicks up behind the
tires.

DEV
(to himself, checking the empty road ahead)
Not a cloud in sight. Good driving weather, at least.
